# Bounded, resumable chapter content review

Measured against the actual saved work, the chapter-review requests for historical chapters 1, 50 and 100 contain 340,184, 446,236 and 529,444 serialized characters. The previous 300,000-character rejection therefore blocked even the opening chapter. The measurement is read-only and does not imply that these historical candidates or their approved plan match proposed plan60.

Large chapter reviews now reuse the established coverage, cross-check, follow-up and synthesis machinery, with an explicit chapter-review purpose and separate prose-review instructions. The complete current chapter, brief and covenant remain shared context. Every byte of every source is retained in raw units, all raw partitions and partition pairs are scheduled, and summaries cannot erase raw revision findings or unresolved insufficiency. Dedicated source requests follow the same verified resolution rules as design review; prose review never records a design approval.

Each completed or failed stage is appended to candidate check history. A restart reconstructs the schedule and validates saved results before resuming; changed input hashes, missing stages or a forged summary cannot authorize adoption. Raw model responses, failures, attempts and evidence remain inspectable. Pause/cancel received during a stage saves that stage and stops before the next model call or adoption. The quality page exposes historical batches and their evidence. Storage continues to use the existing JSON metadata column; no migration or root Prisma regeneration was needed.

Frontend TypeScript and Vite build passed with the existing bundle-size warning.

Neither diagnostic is a full chapter review or a review of proposed plan60. A targeted repair instruction now retains the fragment format; a regression covers that exact error path. No fallback was used; monetary cost is unreported.

Remaining limits:

- Full-source pairwise checking is intentionally conservative and grows with book history. Real chapter 100 currently requires at least 55 stages; selective retrieval or reusable verified state will need careful evidence coverage before scaling to 450 chapters. A first-stage diagnostic does not prove that every real follow-up and synthesis will complete or that the model's literary judgments are calibrated.
- Requests for entire large sources can still exceed a follow-up capacity; the system retains insufficiency rather than cutting evidence or changing the conclusion. Finer source records and long-history retrieval remain next work.
- HTTP chapter checks currently wait for their result; checkpoints are durable and visible through refresh, but a dedicated background chapter-review job interface remains desirable for long runs.
- The original temporary validation checkout lost dependency/package files during verification; the cause is unknown. A fresh isolated copy passed the complete suite and frontend build. The original failures are retained in the validation artifact. The frozen formal runtime also had a missing Prisma entry file, preventing a new reader import; a fresh isolated read-only reader verified protected hashes while the existing formal process continued saving stages. Its runtime was not modified or restarted.
- Production API PID 3668 and formal design60 writer PID 4194 stayed running. The frozen formal runtime is unchanged. No production candidate, approved plan, blocker, adopted event or manuscript was edited. Code changes await deployment after the active formal review; generation remains stopped until design quality is established.
